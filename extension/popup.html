Duofari

Pages that reach under Safari’s side controls

Checking this page…

  • Off by default for web apps that draw their own full-screen interface.

Extend under the controls

  • Widens each one and trims a little from its top and bottom.

  • Photos grow to the full width before you scroll to them. Text below them moves down.

More
  • Safari’s controls in landscape

    Auto extends only background colors in landscape. Choose the side if you know it to extend images too. Pages can’t reach under controls on the left.

  • Stops pages sliding sideways. Turn off to keep double-tap zoom and swiping back from the middle of a page; pages then slide back after you scroll.

Diagnostics 

      
    

  


  
Duofari works on the outer display, where Safari keeps its buttons in a column down the side. When iPhone Duo is open, Safari uses the full width and pages are left as they are.
