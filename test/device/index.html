Checking…

1. Painting under the column

Tap Run paint test. The striped bar should reach the right edge of the screen, under Safari’s controls, with no gap.

Then scroll down and back. If the stripes stop at the controls, WebKit clips overflow at the layout viewport and Duofari can’t extend anything: tell us, with the copied report below.

2. Fixed bars

The test also shows a striped bar fixed to the bottom of the page, lifted into its own layer like Duofari does. It should reach under the controls too, and stay put as you scroll.

3. The other side

This bar is pushed 84 points past the left edge. In portrait that is off the screen whatever WebKit does, so check it in landscape with Safari’s controls on the left (see section 5), where the bar’s left end would sit under the controls. WebKit shouldn’t let pages paint there (it clamps overflow at the start edge), which is why Duofari leaves a column on a page’s start side alone. The page should not scroll left either.

With the controls on the left: do stripes show under them?

4. Sideways scrolling

After the paint test the page is wider than its viewport. Try to drag it sideways in the middle of the page: with Duofari’s “Hold pages steady” on, it shouldn’t move; with it off, it should slide back once you let go. Also try swiping back from the left edge, and double-tapping text.

A long read

  
Some paragraphs, so there is something to scroll. Text like this should always end left of Safari’s controls: Duofari never widens anything that holds text. When the page is extended you can still read every line without anything slipping under the buttons, and links stay tappable.

  
Duofari also leaves alone anything that slides sideways by itself, such as carousels and tab strips, and anything that scrolls inside the page. If you find a page where text moves, or where something is cut off that wasn’t before, the report from that page helps track it down.

  
Pinch to zoom in: Duofari pauses while you’re zoomed, so the page can be panned freely, and picks up again when you zoom back out.
